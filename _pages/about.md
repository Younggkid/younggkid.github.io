I am **Chenyang Liu**, a third-year Ph.D. student at Duke University, advised by [Prof. Matthew Lentz](https://users.cs.duke.edu/~mlentz/) and [Prof. Kartik Nayak](https://users.cs.duke.edu/~kartik/). My research focuses on blockchain systems and consensus protocols. I design new protocols, prove they are correct, and improve their performance in practice.

Before Duke, I received my bachelor's degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I worked with [Prof. Guoxing Chen](https://donnod.github.io) on cache side-channel mitigation and confidential computing.
